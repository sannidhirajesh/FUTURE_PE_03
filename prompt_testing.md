# Prompt Testing and Evaluation

## Project: AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator
**Business:** Bean & Bloom Café  
**Task:** FUTURE_PE_03  
**Track:** Prompt Engineering

---

## 1. Objective

The objective of prompt testing is to evaluate whether the prompts generate useful, relevant, readable, and SEO-friendly content for a fictional coffee shop.

The prompts are designed to generate:
- SEO blog topics
- Blog outlines
- SEO-friendly blog articles
- Content clusters
- Local SEO content

---

## 2. Testing Methodology

Each prompt is evaluated using the following criteria:

1. Relevance to the requested topic.
2. Clarity and readability.
3. Appropriate use of SEO keywords.
4. Adherence to the requested format.
5. Usefulness for the intended audience.
6. Accuracy and avoidance of unsupported business claims.

---

| How to Choose the Perfect Coffee | How to choose coffee | Informational |

### Evaluation
The sample output demonstrates relevant topics, keyword selection, and search intent. The full prompt requests 10 topics.

## 7. Test Case 5: Local SEO Content Generator

### Input Prompt
Generate local SEO content for Bean & Bloom Café, including an SEO title, meta description, local keywords, a business description, and a call to action.

Use placeholders for the city and location.

### Expected Output
Location-focused content that uses placeholders instead of inventing business details.

### Sample Output

**SEO Title:** Bean & Bloom Café | Your Cozy Coffee Spot in [City]

**Meta Description:** Discover delicious coffee and a cozy atmosphere at Bean & Bloom Café in [City].

**Keywords:**
- Café in [City]
- Best coffee shop in [City]
- Cozy café in [City]

### Evaluation
The sample output uses location placeholders and avoids inventing an address or other specific business details.
